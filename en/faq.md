# FAQ

## The reader shows no text

TextMesh Pro's Essential Resources may be missing. Run **Window > TextMeshPro > Import TMP Essential Resources** from the Unity menu.

## Some characters in titles or contents show as boxes

The characters needed for document titles and contents are added to the reader font automatically when a PDF is added to the world. If boxes remain, select **PDFReader System > FolioSystem** in the Hierarchy and click **Reconnect reader** in the Inspector. Characters that the bundled font does not have stay as boxes and are listed in the build log.

## A bake failed

The dialog shows the reason and where the log was saved. Click **Copy log** and send it through the BOOTH shop message.

## The window says there are several readers

Use one reader per world. Keep one and delete the others.

## Can I use scanned PDFs?

Yes. Each page is an image, so sharpness when zoomed depends on the scan resolution, and the world tends to get larger. If you have the original document, a PDF exported from it works better.

## Does it work on Quest (Android)?

No. PDFReader is for VRChat PC worlds.
