# PDFReader Manual

PDFReader is a reader for PDFs in VRChat worlds. You bake PDFs in Unity (convert them into display data), add them to your world, and read them with a mouse on desktop or a controller laser in VR.

## Requirements

- VRChat PC worlds (desktop and PC VR). Android (standalone Quest) and iOS are not supported
- Unity 2022.3.22f1
- Tested with VRChat SDK - Worlds 3.9.0 (a world project created with VCC)
- Baking PDFs requires Windows (x64)

Use one reader per world and switch between PDFs in its library.

The reader cannot open local files or URLs inside VRChat. Bake your PDFs in Unity and add them to the world beforehand.
